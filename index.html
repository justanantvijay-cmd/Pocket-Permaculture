Balcony Tutorial

A quick, one-season taste of companion planting before you head to the land.

One season (~21 days of play) is enough to learn guilds, watering, and harvesting. Skip straight to the land →

❓ How to Play

🎯 Goal

Grow a thriving balcony plot. Plant crops, keep them watered, protect them from pests and weather, then harvest for coins or heirloom seeds.

👆 Actions

Pick a tool from the row below the grid, then tap a tile. 💧 Spray waters a tile. Plant tools buy (or use a saved seed) and plant a crop on an empty tile. Tapping a pest-infested tile clears the bugs. Use 🧺 Process Harvest to collect any crop at full maturity.

🕐 Time

Time doesn't pass on its own — watering, planting, and clearing pests each nudge the clock forward, and every few of those a full day ticks by (weather rolls, crops grow, the market shifts). In a hurry? ⏩ Fast-Forward spends 1 energy to skip straight to the next day.

That's everything you need to start. The rest — guilds, weather, seasons, and more — unlocks here as you go, or tap See the full guide now →

🔁 Regrowth

Basil, tomato, marigold, chili, mint, and okra are cut-and-come-again — harvest them and they stay on the tile, flushing again. Bean and spinach are a one-time flush — harvesting clears the tile and you'll need to replant (they sell for more per harvest to make up for it).

Repeatedly harvesting a regrowing plant depletes its soil — 🍂 next to its moisture % means it's regrowing slower. A neighboring 🫘 bean (Nitrogen Fix) sustains or restores it, so a regrower planted alone will wear out, but one kept near a bean stays productive indefinitely.

✨ Guilds

Plants react to their neighbors, checked in this priority order:

  1. Companion Guild — basil next to tomato (Aroma) or chili (Spice) boosts growth for both.
  2. Nitrogen Fix — 🫘 bean cuts water use and feeds neighboring tiles.
  3. Pest Shield — 🌼 marigold protects neighbors from outbreaks.
  4. Mint-Choked — 🍃 mint grows fast but stunts non-mint neighbors.

🌦️ Weather

☀️ Heatwaves triple evaporation (less for drought-tolerant crops like chili & okra), wilt cold-loving spinach, and spike prices for heat-loving crops. 🌧️ Monsoons fully water your plot but soften prices. Heatwave odds and pest pressure both climb as a season goes on — the back half is tougher than the front, so plan ahead.

🐛 Pests

Outbreaks strike unshielded plants at random, stall their growth, and can set them back a stage. Tap an infested tile to clear it.

📅 Seasons

Each season runs 21 days. Two goals are tracked all season — a coin target and a peak-guilds target, shown just above the grid. At day 21 you get a Harvest Festival recap with a title based on what you hit, and a fresh plot for the next season (coins and saved seeds carry over).

A few 🪨 rocky tiles are off-limits each season, and later seasons have more of them — the same 4x4 grid, less room to work with. Seasons 1 and 2 also unlock new plants: chili & mint arrive after Season 1, okra & spinach after Season 2.

🎁 Foraging

🌿 Forage Neighborhood usually turns up a seed you've already unlocked. Every so often it's a surprise gift instead — a seed for a plant you haven't unlocked yet. It's saved for you and shows up the moment that plant becomes available.

📈 Market

Selling the same crop over and over floods the market and temporarily depresses its own price — diversifying (and leaning on guild pairings) keeps your returns healthier than monoculture.

🏙️ Cities

Mumbai, Phoenix, and London each carry different monsoon/heatwave odds, evaporation rates, and growth speed. Use 🔁 Change City anytime to relocate — your plot resets, but coins, saved seeds, and season progress carry over.
